# LzyDownloader 1.2.59

**More reliable sign-in recovery:** Video and audio downloads can now start
without configured browser cookies and retry once with them when yt-dlp reports
an explicit sign-in, age verification, membership, or CAPTCHA requirement.

**Clearer startup checks:** A non-modal progress window now appears while
installed tools are checked, with app-managed updates identified as they start.
The bundled yt-dlp and gallery-dl extractor catalogs are refreshed for this
release.
